About Me

I'm Jerome Padilla, a passionate Computer Science student at Vanier College with a strong interest in both programming and design. My journey in technology began with hardware, repairing and building computers, which naturally evolved into software development and digital design.

I specialize in creating clean, functional websites using modern web technologies. My technical skills include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and Java. I've developed several projects including a Java-based train ticketing system and a responsive website called Thinkflow, which helped me develop both my technical and design skills.

Beyond coding, I have experience with design tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva. I believe good design is as important as good code in creating exceptional digital experiences. My approach combines logical problem-solving with creative design thinking.

Currently, I'm focused on expanding my knowledge of programming languages for computer science while continuing to develop my skills. I'm always looking for new challenges that allow me to grow as a developer.
