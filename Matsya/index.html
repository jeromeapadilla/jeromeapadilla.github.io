Our story

Born from two beautiful rituals.

Matsya is our love letter to the Filipino merienda and the Japanese way of tea.

Why Matsya?

A name that brings
two worlds together.

“Matsya” carries the familiar sound of matcha while feeling at home in a Filipino conversation. We wanted a name for the place where Japan’s quiet tea ritual meets the warmth and community of the Filipino taho vendor.

Our cups layer fresh silken tofu, chewy sago, and slow-cooked arnibal with stone-ground Uji matcha and flavours we grew up loving—from ube to mango and calamansi.

Para sa bawat merienda. For every afternoon pause.
